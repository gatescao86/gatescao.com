I wanted a place to think in public — slowly, in full sentences, without the shape of a feed.

The first notes will probably be about math, physics, computer science, and philosophy, because those are the rooms I keep walking back into. Other rooms will show up too: tennis, cello, languages, work, the ordinary weather of a life.

This is a commonplace book, not a brand. I'll add pages when I have something to put on them.
